Who We Are

About Bluefield Academy

Founded in 2009, Bluefield Academy has grown into one of Monrovia's most respected schools. We offer quality education from nursery through senior secondary level in a safe, nurturing environment.

Our mission is to develop well-rounded individuals who are academically strong, morally grounded, and ready to lead in a changing world.

  • ✓  Small class sizes for personal attention
  • ✓  Experienced and certified teaching staff
  • ✓  Modern classrooms and learning resources
  • ✓  Active sports and extracurricular programmes
